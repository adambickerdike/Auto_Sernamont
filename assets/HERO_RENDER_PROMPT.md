# Photoreal hero render — prompt

`assets/hero.png` is a computed information graphic: it is generated by
[`tools/fig_hero.py`](../tools/fig_hero.py) from the same Jones-calculus model
the analysis uses, so the polarisation ellipse drawn beside each station is the
real state at that point in the beam.

If you would rather have a photoreal render of the bench at the top of the
README, the prompt below is the one to use. Save the result as
`assets/hero_render.png` and swap the `<img>` source in `README.md`.

```text
A hyperrealistic, cinematic vertical 3D render and photograph of a Sénarmont
polarimetry optical experiment inside a cleanroom laboratory.

Composition & Layout: Vertical 9:16 orientation. A vertical optical cage
assembly is mounted on a matte black anodized optical breadboard with visible
M6 threaded grid holes.

Top section: An industrial CMOS scientific camera is mounted at the top looking
down into an optical non-polarizing beamsplitter cube. To the top-right, an
infrared diode laser head emits a visible, pencil-thin crimson guide beam
directed via two precision silver turning mirrors into the side port of the
beamsplitter.

Upper optical path: From the beamsplitter downward, the beam passes through a
rotatable manual linear polarizer in a graduated mount, a precision half-wave
plate (λ/2) in a calibrated rotation dial, and an objective focusing lens.

Center stage: A microfabricated barium titanate (BTO) thin-film photonic chip
rests on a high-precision multi-axis translation stage. Two sharp gold-plated
micro-positioner probe tips make direct contact with miniature gold coplanar
electrodes on the chip surface. The laser focuses to an intense, micron-scale
red spot directly in the narrow gap between the gold electrodes. Flexible
micro-coaxial cables snake outward from the probe arms toward a stack of
rack-mounted lab electronics off to the right (Source Measure Unit, RF signal
generator, lock-in amplifier, and digital oscilloscope with glowing numeric
screens and active waveforms).

Lower optical path: Directly beneath the transparent substrate mount, the
transmitted beam passes through a collection collimation lens, a quarter-wave
plate (λ/4) in a calibrated dial, an analyzing linear polarizer (polarizer 2),
and a focusing lens, terminating directly into a high-speed photodetector at
the bottom.

Visual style & Details: Scientific laboratory aesthetic, hyper-detailed,
cleanroom lighting with moody ambient laboratory illumination, subtle red laser
bloom in transparent glass optics, antireflection coatings displaying faint
magenta and green optical reflections on lens surfaces, realistic brushed
metal, black anodized aluminum mounts, depth of field focused sharply on the
microchip gap and probe needles, 8k resolution, octane render, photojournalistic
scientific photography --ar 9:16 --style raw
```

> The render is decoration. The physics in the documentation comes from
> [`assets/setup_schematic.png`](setup_schematic.png) — the real instrument
> diagram — and from the computed figures in [`assets/figures/`](figures/).
